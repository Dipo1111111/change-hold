# Scope

Primary surface: dashboard (the app as a whole — its screens are pages of the same book).

## Visitor Mode

Operate — a personal, phone-first record of change owed by Nigerian shops, captured in under 15 seconds with taps only.

## Audience / Job / Task

One person, one phone, both daylight and dim rooms. They walk away from a shop counter owing forgotten change; they log it in seconds with a 6-step tap wizard, and later read the record back to the vendor as an un-arguable claim.

## Proof / Content / Constraints

- Every balance is a concrete, checkable story: exact item, price, notes handed, notes returned.
- Zero keyboard in the log flow. No price catalogs. Settled entries stay in the book (struck through, never deleted).
- Scenes: bright Lagos daylight AND dim rooms — the light paper page must hold high contrast in both.
- All incumbent functionality preserved.

## Direction contract

THESIS: ChangeHold is the vendor's own debt book: a market ledger written in biro, where the red total is the first thing the eye lands on and every claim reads like the trader's own evidence. It refuses every clean fintech-banking money dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Aged ivory ruled paper on a dark desk; blue-black biro ink for structure, a single committed red ink for money owed, pencil for marginalia. Settled = red strike-through plus a VOID stamp; the double-ruled total; brass notes. Tabular numerals everywhere.

STORY: The user sees at a glance what is written against them, and can open a shop's account and read the page word-for-word to a vendor who honors exactly this format.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A ruled ledger page floating on the desk. Page-head rules with a small caps column label; the hero total inked in red, single-ruled above, double-ruled beneath like a book total. Three shop accounts as ruled rows — biro name, red balance, pencil "x open change", a stamp when owed· Settled rows strike through and stamp VOID. One-off is its own ruled stub. Primary action is the red "＋" ink stamp, bottom-center.

FORM: Market ledger notation (paper/notation family, position 1 of seven). Seed key 7d4353c9.

FINISH: the run's exit condition: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
